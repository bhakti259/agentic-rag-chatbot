GATTU, CHINKI AND THEIR FRIENDS
- BY MIHIT
1 DAY THERE WAS STORM AND 2 KIDS NAMED GATTU AND CHINKI HAD SCHOOL THEN THEIR PAPA  TOOK THEM FROM THE CAR TO SCHOOL.THEN GATTU AND CHINKI  MET GUDDI HARI AND BUNTY AND THERE WAS EVEN MONTY WHICH GATTU AND CHINKI DID NOT LIKE AT ALL.IT WAS GATTU AND CHINKI’S EXAM DAY.AND GATTU FORGOT SOMETHING AT HOME. AND GATTU CHINKI AND THEIR FRIEND NAMED BUNTY CAME IN THE CLASSROOM AND THEN THE TEACHER CAME IN THE CLASSROOM.AND THE CLASS TEACHER GAVE THEM EXAM SHEETS.AND THE TEACHER TOLD CHINKI TO SHARE HER PENCIL WITH GATTU.AND THEN MONTY STARTED ASKING GUDDY IF THE QUESTION NUMBER B3’S ANSWER IS B4 OR 42.THEN MONTY STARTED ASKING CHINKI WHAT IS THE ANSWER OF QUESTION NUMBER B3.AND THEN MONTY STARTED SAYING PLEASE, PLEASE, PLEASE.AND THEN THE CLASS TEACHER CAME NEAR MONTY’S DESK WITH ANGRY FACE. SHE ASKED MONTY –“WHY ARE YOU TALKING?” AND THEN MONTY SMILED AND SAID-“ THIS IS MY LAST CHANCE.” AND THEN THE CLASS TEACHER SAID “MONTY YOU ALWAYS DO THIS.” “STAND UP” SAID THE CLASS TEACHER.AND THEN THE TEACHER SAID “MONTY, YOU LIKE TO STAND UP.SO GO OUT OF THE CLASSROOM AND STAND THERE.”
AND THEN GATTU CHINKI , GUDDI HARI BUNTY, MONTY AND THE OTHER KIDS IN GATTU CHINKI’S CLASS EVEN WENT HOME. GATTU CHINKI ARRIVED AT THEIR HOME .THERE WERE GATTU CHINKI’S MOM AND DAD.GATTU CHINKI’S  DAD ASKED “DID THE EXAM WENT WELL?” THEN CHINKI SAID TO THEIR MOM AND DAD THAT IT WAS NOT SO GOOD.CHINKI ALSO SAID THAT THERE WAS NO TIME AND SHE COULD NOT WRITE 1 SENTENCE.AND THEN THE DOOR BELL RANG, CHINKI OPENED THE DOOR.SHE SAW, IT WAS MONTY.MONTY SAID TO CHINKI’S DAD-“SORRY, UNCLE.NEXT TIME I WILL TRY TO NOT CHEAT”. THEN GATTU CHINKI’S DAD SAID-“NO CHEATING???”.MONTY SAID –“THAT MEANS I WILL NOT CHEAT “. AND THEN CHINKI LAUGHED - “HEHEHEHEH”.
